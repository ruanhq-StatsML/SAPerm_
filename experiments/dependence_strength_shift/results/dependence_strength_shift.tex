\title{Dual ranking for dependence shift:\\ strength $A_j$ and PO-risk $\Delta^{\mathrm{PO}}_j$}
\author{Applied note --- SAPerm / OOD VIMP}
\date{}
\maketitle

\noindent\textit{Direct application.} Given two batches (or factor windows), fit one
conditional mean per feature and emit \textbf{two} rankings: dependence-strength
shift $A_j$ (signed redundancy) and PO-risk gap $\Delta^{\mathrm{PO}}_j$
(relationship / map change, including flips). Ranking recovery shows they are not
interchangeable. On factor panels, run the same scores on residuals after removing $G$.

\section{Two scores from one fit}

For coordinate $X_j$ and batch $t\in\{0,1\}$, fit $m_{t,j}=\widehat{\mathbb{E}}_t[X_j\mid X_{-j}]$.
Held-out correlation ratio and signed strength shift:
\begin{equation}
\eta^{2}_{t,j}=1-\frac{\mathrm{MSE}_t(X_j,m_{t,j})}{\widehat{\mathrm{Var}}_t(X_j)},
\qquad
A_j=\eta^{2}_{1,j}-\eta^{2}_{0,j}.
\label{eq:Aj}
\end{equation}
$A_j>0$: peers explain more of $X_j$ on the target (redundancy up).
$A_j<0$: dependence weakened.

Same fits, PO-risk ranking. With propensity $\pi$, contrast $\tau_j=m_{1,j}-m_{0,j}$,
and doubly robust pseudo-outcome $\phi$ (batch as treatment),
\begin{equation}
R(\tau)=\widehat{\mathbb{E}}[(\phi-\tau)^2],
\qquad
\Delta^{\mathrm{PO}}_j=R(0)-R(\tau_j).
\label{eq:PO}
\end{equation}
Rank by $\Delta^{\mathrm{PO}}_j$ to find where the \emph{conditional map} moved.
Large PO-risk does \emph{not} mean stronger dependence: a pure sign flip has $A_j\approx 0$
and large $\Delta^{\mathrm{PO}}_j$.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Ranking & Score & Question \\
\midrule
Strength & $A_j$ \eqref{eq:Aj} & Intensity up/down (signed) \\
PO-risk & $\Delta^{\mathrm{PO}}_j$ \eqref{eq:PO} & Map changed? (incl.\ flip) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\section{Factor residuals (quant application)}

Write $F_j=\beta_j^\top G+\varepsilon_j$. Apply \eqref{eq:Aj}--\eqref{eq:PO} to the residual
panel $(\varepsilon_1,\ldots,\varepsilon_p)$ across two windows:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item $A_j(\varepsilon)$: idiosyncratic crowding / diversification.
\item $\Delta^{\mathrm{PO}}_j(\varepsilon)$: residual hedge / conditional-map flip.
\item Optional $V_{j\leftarrow k}$: which partner residual's unique $R^2$ changed.
\end{itemize}
Pairwise $\mathrm{Corr}(\varepsilon_j,\varepsilon_k)$ is the linear special case.
API: \texttt{factor\_residual\_dependence\_shift(F\_ref, F\_new, n\_common=1)}.

Spearman$(A)=\mathrm{corr}(\mathrm{rank}(A^{\mathrm{oracle}}),\mathrm{rank}(\mathrm{score}))$.
Strength should recover the signed ladder; PO-risk should not (it over-ranks flips).

\section{How to read the two rankings}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lllp{5.8cm}}
\toprule
$A_j$ & $\Delta^{\mathrm{PO}}$ & Pattern & Action \\
\midrule
$\downarrow$ large & small--mid & Diversification & Peers explain less; residual risk more unique \\
$\uparrow$ large & small--mid & Crowding & Residuals more reconstructible \\
$\approx 0$ & $\uparrow$ large & Map / hedge flip & Re-check hedges, not only variance \\
large & large & Both moved & Redesign intensity and relationship \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Ranking recovery's insight is the \emph{gap}: mean UCI Spearman$(A)$ is 
0.879 for strength vs 0.289 for PO-risk. 
Use both; neither replaces the other.
