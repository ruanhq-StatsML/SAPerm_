\section{Higher-order PO interaction and LOMO cross-validation}
\paragraph{From combo power to fusion policy.}
Combo rejection rates say which modality stack detects a shift;
they do not say whether triple early fusion is efficient.
We therefore report the inclusion--exclusion PO residual $I^{(3)}$
together with leave-one-modality-out (LOMO) power gaps as a
\emph{cross-validation} of the fusion diagnosis
(sign agreement; not unique modality attribution).
Outcome / $\tau$ / $\nu$ models are Random Forests
(\texttt{rf\_regressor}), matching the CF/DR/RF power board.
