Writing $\Delta_{\mathrm{PO}}(\mathcal{S})$ for the PO-risk increment of modality
subset $\mathcal{S}$, the three-way interaction is the inclusion--exclusion residual
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:I3-delta-po}
\begin{aligned}
I^{(3)}(M_1,M_2,M_3)
&=
\Delta_{\mathrm{PO}}(M_1,M_2,M_3)
-
\sum_{i<j}\Delta_{\mathrm{PO}}(M_i,M_j)
+
\sum_{i}\Delta_{\mathrm{PO}}(M_i)\,.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Equivalently, with leave-out PO risk $R(S)$ (held-out PO after dropping the
feature blocks in $S$),
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:I3-leaveout}
I^{(3)}
=
\sum_{i} R(\{i\})
-
\sum_{i<j} R(\{i,j\})
+
R(\{1,2,3\})\,.
\end{equation}
Pairwise interactions use
$I^{(2)}=R(\{m_1\})+R(\{m_2\})-R(\{m_1,m_2\})$, and the calibrated readout is
$I_{\mathrm{cal}}^{(k)}=I^{(k)}-\mathrm{PO}(\mathrm{full})$.
Complementing the residual, leave-one-modality-out (LOMO) power
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:lomo-power}
\Delta^{\mathrm{pow}}_{m}
=
\mathrm{Power}(\mathrm{TAV})
-
\mathrm{Power}(\mathrm{TAV}\setminus\{m\})
\end{equation}
provides a deployment-facing \emph{cross-validation} of $\mathrm{sign}(I^{(3)})$:
$\Delta^{\mathrm{pow}}_{m}<0$ means early-concat triple fusion is stronger without
modality $m$ (over-complete / conflicting), and should line up with
$I^{(3)}_{\mathrm{cal}}<0$.
We emphasize that LOMO is a sign-level cross-check of the fusion diagnosis,
not a second unique attribution of modality shares.

\paragraph{Empirical reading.}
Taken together, $I^{(2)}$, $I^{(3)}$ (Table~\ref{tab:I2-I3-rf}) and LOMO
power gaps (Table~\ref{tab:lomo-power-cv}) simply characterize how modalities
interact under the shift:
which pairs carry the signal, whether the third channel adds non-additive
structure, and whether early-concat TAV improves on the best pair.
Under concept drift we observe
$\mathrm{Power}(T{\times}A)>\mathrm{Power}(\mathrm{TAV})$
(Table~\ref{tab:full-benchmark-power-typeI}),
\texttt{vs\_best}$<0$, and $I_{\mathrm{cal}}^{(3)}<0$ on both PCA bands---i.e.,
the interaction among text~/~audio~/~video is not super-additive here.
LOMO is only a sign-level check of that interaction profile, not a unique
attribution of each modality.
