The natural inputs are three nested comparisons:
(i)~which \emph{modality} contributes
($\Delta_{\mathrm{PO}}(M_i)$ / single-modality power);
(ii)~which \emph{subset} contributes
($\Delta_{\mathrm{PO}}(\mathcal{S})$ for pairs and TAV);
(iii)~which modality has the largest \emph{marginal} contribution when dropped
from a fixed full system (LOMO).
Higher-order interactions are read off these increments, nothing more exotic.

Writing $\Delta_{\mathrm{PO}}(\mathcal{S})$ for the PO-risk increment of modality
subset $\mathcal{S}$, the three-way interaction is the inclusion--exclusion residual
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:I3-delta-po}
\begin{aligned}
I^{(3)}(M_1,M_2,M_3)
&=
\Delta_{\mathrm{PO}}(M_1,M_2,M_3)
-
\sum_{i<j}\Delta_{\mathrm{PO}}(M_i,M_j)
+
\sum_{i}\Delta_{\mathrm{PO}}(M_i)\,.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Equivalently, with leave-out PO risk $R(S)$ (held-out PO after dropping the
feature blocks in $S$),
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:I3-leaveout}
I^{(3)}
=
\sum_{i} R(\{i\})
-
\sum_{i<j} R(\{i,j\})
+
R(\{1,2,3\})\,.
\end{equation}
Pairwise interactions use
$I^{(2)}=R(\{m_1\})+R(\{m_2\})-R(\{m_1,m_2\})$, and the calibrated readout is
$I_{\mathrm{cal}}^{(k)}=I^{(k)}-\mathrm{PO}(\mathrm{full})$.
The LOMO power gap
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:lomo-power}
\Delta^{\mathrm{pow}}_{m}
=
\mathrm{Power}(\mathrm{TAV})
-
\mathrm{Power}(\mathrm{TAV}\setminus\{m\})
\end{equation}
is a sign-level check of the same interaction profile
($\Delta^{\mathrm{pow}}_{m}<0$ $\Leftrightarrow$ early-concat is stronger without $m$),
not a unique attribution of modality shares.

\paragraph{Empirical reading.}
These tables answer the three natural questions under the shift.
\emph{Subset:} combo power ranks T$\times$A $>$ TAV $>$ T$\times$V for CF/DR on
top-30 (Table~\ref{tab:full-benchmark-power-typeI});
\texttt{vs\_best}$<0$ throughout Table~\ref{tab:lomo-power-cv}.
\emph{Modality / marginal:} dropping video from TAV raises CF/DR/RF power on
top-30; on the mid-band, dropping audio (keeping T$\times$V) hurts least /
helps most.
\emph{Interaction:} $I_{\mathrm{cal}}^{(2)}$ and $I_{\mathrm{cal}}^{(3)}$ are
negative on concept and on MOSEI (Table~\ref{tab:I2-I3-rf}); under planted
$\pm\lambda$, the antagonistic plant yields a more negative pairwise
$I_{\mathrm{cal}}^{(2)}$ on the planted pair than the synergistic plant.
Null losses stay at $0$ and calibrated signs stay near quiet.
Altogether: the cross-modal interaction is readable from modality, subset,
and marginal increments; here it is not super-additive under early-concat TAV.
