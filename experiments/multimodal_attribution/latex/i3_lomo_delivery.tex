\paragraph{Definitions.}
Writing $\Delta_{\mathrm{PO}}(\mathcal{S})$ for the PO-risk increment of modality
subset $\mathcal{S}$, the three-way interaction is the inclusion--exclusion residual
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:delivery-I3}
\begin{aligned}
I^{(3)}(M_1,M_2,M_3)
&=
\Delta_{\mathrm{PO}}(M_1,M_2,M_3)
-
\sum_{i<j}\Delta_{\mathrm{PO}}(M_i,M_j)
+
\sum_{i}\Delta_{\mathrm{PO}}(M_i).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
With leave-out PO risk $R(S)$ (held-out PO after dropping blocks in $S$),
equivalently
$I^{(3)}=\sum_i R(\{i\})-\sum_{i<j} R(\{i,j\})+R(\{1,2,3\})$.
Pairwise terms use
$I^{(2)}=R(\{m_1\})+R(\{m_2\})-R(\{m_1,m_2\})$ and the calibrated readout
$I_{\mathrm{cal}}^{(k)}=I^{(k)}-\mathrm{PO}(\mathrm{full})$.
Leave-one-modality-out (LOMO) power
$\Delta^{\mathrm{pow}}_{m}
=\mathrm{Power}(\mathrm{TAV})-\mathrm{Power}(\mathrm{TAV}\setminus\{m\})$
is the deployment-facing cross-validation of $\mathrm{sign}(I^{(3)})$:
loss $<0$ means early-concat triple fusion is stronger without $m$
(over-complete / conflicting), matching $I^{(3)}_{\mathrm{cal}}<0$.

\paragraph{Delivery conclusion.}
Under synthetic concept drift, combo power alone already ranks
$\mathrm{Power}(T{\times}A)>\mathrm{Power}(\mathrm{TAV})$ (CF/DR/RF;
Table~\ref{tab:delivery-lomo}).
LOMO makes the inefficiency explicit: every primary method has
\texttt{vs\_best}$<0$, and dropping video from TAV \emph{raises} CF/DR/RF power
on top-30 --- the third channel is over-complete under early concat.
RF PO residuals cross-validate that diagnosis:
$I_{\mathrm{cal}}^{(2)}<0$ and $I_{\mathrm{cal}}^{(3)}<0$ on both PCA bands
(Table~\ref{tab:delivery-I2-I3}), while the null board stays near-quiet on
calibrated signs (frac $I_{\mathrm{cal}}>0\approx 0$).
Statistically, $I^{(3)}$ is a M\"obius residual (not a unique causal share);
LOMO is a fixed-caliber power contrast.
Practically, the reconciled pair says: ship the dominant pair
(T$\times$A on top-30; T$\times$V on mid-band), treat the third modality as
optional audit~/~late fusion, and do \emph{not} default to triple early concat.
Agreement in sign between $I_{\mathrm{cal}}^{(3)}$ and LOMO \texttt{vs\_best}
is the delivery cross-validation; magnitude equality is not claimed.
