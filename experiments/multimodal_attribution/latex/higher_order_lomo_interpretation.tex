\paragraph{Higher-order interaction and leave-one-modality-out power.}
One step further, we quantify how efficiently modalities interact for
domain~/~label characterization.
Writing $\Delta_{\mathrm{PO}}(\mathcal{S})$ for the PO-risk increment
attributable to modality subset $\mathcal{S}$, the three-way interaction
among modalities $M_1,M_2,M_3$ (e.g.\ text~/~audio~/~video) is the
inclusion--exclusion residual
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:I3-delta-po}
\begin{aligned}
I^{(3)}(M_1,M_2,M_3)
&=
\Delta_{\mathrm{PO}}(M_1,M_2,M_3)
-
\Delta_{\mathrm{PO}}(M_1,M_2)
-
\Delta_{\mathrm{PO}}(M_1,M_3)
-
\Delta_{\mathrm{PO}}(M_2,M_3)\\
&\qquad
+
\Delta_{\mathrm{PO}}(M_1)
+
\Delta_{\mathrm{PO}}(M_2)
+
\Delta_{\mathrm{PO}}(M_3).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Equivalently, with leave-out PO risk $R(S)$ denoting held-out PO risk after
dropping the feature blocks in $S$,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:I3-leaveout}
I^{(3)}
=
\sum_{i} R(\{i\})
-
\sum_{i<j} R(\{i,j\})
+
R(\{1,2,3\}),
\end{equation}
so that $I^{(3)}$ isolates the non-additive remainder beyond all single- and
pairwise PO increments.
Complementing the residual, the leave-one-modality-out (LOMO) power gap
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:lomo-power}
\Delta^{\mathrm{pow}}_{m}
:=
\mathrm{Power}(\mathrm{full})
-
\mathrm{Power}(\mathrm{full}\setminus\{m\})
\end{equation}
gives a direct, deployment-facing readout of which channel moves detection
most: $\Delta^{\mathrm{pow}}_{m}>0$ means dropping $m$ hurts power
(modality helps the joint test);
$\Delta^{\mathrm{pow}}_{m}<0$ means the joint system is stronger without $m$
(redundancy or conflict under early fusion).
The two measures are designed to \emph{reconcile}:
$I^{(3)}>0$ predicts jointly positive LOMO gaps
(every channel earns its seat in the triple);
$I^{(3)}\approx 0$ predicts near-zero gaps once the dominant pair is kept
(essentially pairwise mechanisms);
$I^{(3)}<0$ predicts at least one non-positive gap---and typically
$\mathrm{Power}(\mathrm{full})<\max_{i<j}\mathrm{Power}(\{i,j\})$---i.e.\
over-complete or conflicting triple fusion.

\paragraph{Statistical reading.}
Statistically, $I^{(3)}$ is a M\"obius residual on the PO-risk set function:
it is not a unique causal share of any one modality, but a scalar test of
whether the three-block leave-out pattern is super-additive, additive, or
sub-additive after removing all lower-order terms.
Under a calibrated null (type-I of pairwise $\texttt{interact\_PO}$ near
$\alpha$), a stable sign of $I^{(3)}$ is interpretable as a property of the
decision shift rather than a size artifact.
LOMO gaps $\Delta^{\mathrm{pow}}_{m}$ are Monte Carlo power contrasts at
fixed $(n,B,n_{\mathrm{perm}},\alpha)$; they inherit the usual Monte Carlo
uncertainty of power estimates and are path-dependent on the chosen full
system (triple vs.\ best pair as baseline).
Reconciliation is therefore qualitative and sign-based
(joint positivity~/~negativity of $I^{(3)}$ and the LOMO profile),
not a claim that $|I^{(3)}|$ equals any single $\Delta^{\mathrm{pow}}_{m}$.
When the outcome nuisance models are Random Forests
(\texttt{rf\_regressor} for $m,\tau,\nu$), both $I^{(3)}$ and the CF/DR/RF
power board share the same learner class, so disagreements are attributable
to \emph{what} is measured (PO residual vs.\ rejection rate), not to a
model mismatch.

\paragraph{Practical reading.}
In practice, the pair $(I^{(3)},\{\Delta^{\mathrm{pow}}_{m}\})$ turns
``which combo has power'' into a fusion policy:
\begin{itemize}
  \item $I^{(3)}>0$ and $\Delta^{\mathrm{pow}}_{m}>0$ for all $m$
  $\Rightarrow$ keep joint three-channel fusion (true complementary cues).
  \item $I^{(3)}\approx 0$ with one large pairwise power and near-zero LOMO
  gaps for the third channel
  $\Rightarrow$ ship the dominant pair; treat the third modality as optional
  audit~/~gate.
  \item $I^{(3)}<0$ or $\Delta^{\mathrm{pow}}_{m}<0$ for some $m$
  $\Rightarrow$ early-concat triple fusion is over-complete or conflicting:
  drop or late-fuse the harmful channel, or replace concat by a conflict
  gate---exactly the pattern already visible when
  $\mathrm{Power}(T{\times}A)>\mathrm{Power}(\mathrm{TAV})$ under concept
  drift.
\end{itemize}
Thus $I^{(3)}$ diagnoses \emph{interaction efficiency} of the PO increments,
while LOMO power names \emph{which} modality to keep or cut; together they
convert multimodal attribution into an actionable fusion decision without
pretending to a unique percentage attribution of each channel.
