\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Adding meta-features $M$ to embeddings $Z$ is not only about raising power.
The \emph{joint} pattern of detection power change and PO-risk ratio
\[
\rho_{\mathrm{PO}}
=\frac{\mathrm{PO}(Z{+}M)}{\mathrm{PO}(Z)},
\qquad
\mathrm{PO}=\mathbb{E}[\tau(X)^2],\ 
\tau\approx(Y-\mu)(W-e)
\]
is \textbf{statistical evidence of embed$\times$meta interaction}---not a bug
to patch. Different DGPs leave different signatures; that is the scientific
payload.
\end{abstract}

\section{Claim}
RFPerm / DRPerm / CFPerm on $X=[Z_{\mathrm{PCA}},M]$ with named block indices
yield a closed loop: detect $\to$ type $\to$ localize.
The upgrade in this iteration: treat
$(\Delta\mathrm{Power},\,\rho_{\mathrm{PO}})$ as an \emph{interaction
signature}, mutually readable with CFPerm block power.

\paragraph{Reading (interaction, not repair).}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Absorb}: $\rho_{\mathrm{PO}}\ll 1$, DR power stays $\approx 0$.
        $M$ enters the nuisance $(\mu,e)$; embed$\times$meta interaction
        \emph{removes} residual batch--outcome dependence.
  \item \textbf{Localize}: CFPerm $\Delta>0$ on the meta block while DR may
        stay quiet---interaction site is the named block (syntax / sentiment),
        visible to LOCO even when PO channel is absorbed.
  \item \textbf{Concept residual}: $\rho_{\mathrm{PO}}\approx 1$ and DR/RF at
        ceiling---interaction cannot absorb the shift; CFPerm still shows
        \emph{where} meta helps attribute it ($+0.23$ on heldout).
\end{itemize}

\section{Why this is stronger than ``power went up''}
A lone power increase is ambiguous (easier alternative vs.\ worse Type-I).
A lone PO-risk drop is also ambiguous (underfitting $\tau$).
Together with CFPerm on list-indices:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Power says whether we reject more often.
  \item $\rho_{\mathrm{PO}}$ says whether $M$ changed the \emph{channel}.
  \item Block CFPerm says \emph{which modality coordinates} carry the interaction.
\end{enumerate}
That triad is the interaction evidence.
