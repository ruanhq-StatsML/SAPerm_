\section*{Statistical reading}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Type-I.} On a random split, RF/DR/RR, MMD, C2ST, CFPerm,
        kernel MMD, and Miles sit near $\alpha{=}0.05$. Chen--Qin does not.
  \item \textbf{Concept drift is an outcome shift.}
        $P(X)$ tests stay near $\alpha$ ($\pi$ flat, MMD$_X{=}0.01$),
        while RF/DR/RR power is $1$. CFPerm misses text
        (image $.79$, audio $.70$, text $.00$).
        $\rho_{\mathrm{PO}}{\approx}0.91$ is residual, not absorb.
  \item \textbf{Text sentiment is a detectable covariate on the copy channel.}
        $\pi_{\mathrm{text}}{=}0.91$. Two-sample tests on $X$ and $(X,Y)$
        and CF$_{\mathrm{text}}$ all have power $1$.
        DRPerm power is $.02$: the VADER block absorbs the batch effect
        out of the residual outcome. $\rho_{\mathrm{PO}}{>}1$ is not absorption.
  \item \textbf{Image / audio are silent covariates for PO / CF.}
        $\pi$ concentrates on the designed channel ($.95$), and permutation
        MMD, C2ST, and Miles have power $1$, but RF/DR/RR and every CFPerm
        block stay at $\alpha$. The channel moves; it does not become a
        rejectable outcome or block-localization event.
        Kernel MMD is $.05$ on image and $.39$ on audio.
  \item \textbf{Coordinate-wise mean tests} (Xu, Bai, Srivastava) miss every
        covariate window. Chen--Qin alarms everywhere, including the null.
\end{itemize}
