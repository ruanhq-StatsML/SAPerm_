\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Primary object is the PO-risk ratio
$\rho_{\mathrm{PO}}=\mathrm{PO}(X_{\mathrm{rich}})/\mathrm{PO}(X_{\mathrm{base}})$
together with detection power. Direction of $\rho$ (absorb / residual) plus
CFPerm block power (localize) is the whole story. Feature builders
(spaCy+VADER, OpenCV) are fixed inputs.
\end{abstract}

\section{$\rho_{\mathrm{PO}}$ first}
\[
\rho_{\mathrm{PO}}=\frac{\mathrm{PO}(X_{\mathrm{rich}})}{\mathrm{PO}(X_{\mathrm{base}})},
\quad
\mathrm{PO}=\mathbb{E}[\tau(X)^2],\
\tau\approx(Y-\mu)(W-e).
\]
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{llcl}
\toprule
$\rho$ & Power & Name & Reading \\
\midrule
$\ll 1$ & DR stays low & \textbf{absorb} & rich $X$ eats the covariate channel \\
$\approx 1$ & RF/DR high & \textbf{residual} & shift orthogonal to the add-on \\
any & $\Delta$CF${>}0$ & \textbf{localize} & named block carries the interaction \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\paragraph{Block-level ranking (what the table says).}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \texttt{syntax\_meta}: largest CF is
        $\texttt{spacy\_syntax}\,(0.32)\approx\texttt{meta}\,(0.31)
        \gg \texttt{embed\_pca}\,(0.16)$.
        The shift was routed by \texttt{verb\_noun\_ratio}; LOCO recovers
        that \emph{syntax} block, not sentiment or anonymous PCA.
  \item \texttt{sentiment\_meta}: all CF blocks stay near Type-I
        ($0.01$--$0.06$) while $\rho_{\mathrm{PO}}{=}0.25$.
        Contribution is not ``who rejects louder''---$M$ already absorbed
        the polarity channel into $(\mu,e)$.
  \item \texttt{heldout}: \texttt{embed\_pca}\,{=}\,0.96 and
        \texttt{meta}\,{=}\,0.99 both saturate; residual lives in $Z$,
        meta co-localizes but does not own the concept shift.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Why these meta-features (argument).}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{VADER subset} (\texttt{compound}/\texttt{neg}/\texttt{polar\_gap}/\texttt{neu}):
        the sentiment window is defined on \texttt{compound}; carrier
        mass on \texttt{neg} and \texttt{polar\_gap} means the batch gap
        is negative escalation and pos--neg conflict, not length or NER.
        That is the meta problem being solved: \emph{named emotion
        coordinates}, so ops can threshold negative mass without reading
        PCA dims.
  \item \textbf{Syntax subset} (\texttt{verb\_noun\_ratio} + POS):
        the hard DGP flips POS under length match; CFPerm's top block is
        exactly \texttt{spacy\_syntax}. The meta problem is
        \emph{structure drift}: verb-heavy vs noun-heavy prose, not
        polarity.
  \item \textbf{Non-drivers}: \texttt{spacy\_ner}, discourse surface, and
        trailing PCA stay low under sentiment/syntax routing---so the
        claim is not ``any meta helps,'' but ``the aligned block and its
        subset do.''
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Dimension contribution.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Text-routed shift: CF \texttt{text\_meta} saturates ($1.00$) with
        importance $\gg$ image ($0.54$) and audio ($0$ in the tri-modal
        drill). The contributing dimension is the \textbf{text block}.
  \item Image-routed shift: detection RF stays near $0$; $\rho{=}0.62$
        shows image meta \textbf{absorbs} when folded into $X$. Carrier
        subset still names visual coords
        (\texttt{brightness}/\texttt{contrast}/\texttt{sat}/\texttt{hue\_std})
       ---the meta problem is \emph{visual atmosphere}, not copy emotion.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Meta-feature argument (business-readable).}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \texttt{vader\_neg}/\texttt{compound}/\texttt{polar\_gap}:
        negative escalation and conflict on the copy channel $\Rightarrow$
        page the text/ops team; do not retrain vision.
  \item \texttt{img\_brightness}/\texttt{contrast}/\texttt{sat\_mean}:
        luminance and palette drift $\Rightarrow$ visual QA; CFPerm
        prevents a false ``sentiment incident'' on text.
  \item Audio energy windows were weak in the small drill; loudness
        features (\texttt{aud\_rms\_energy}, \ldots) remain the intended
        knobs when that axis is powered.
\end{itemize}

\section{POC: mask image patches (side)}
Zero $50\%$ of $4{\times}4$ patches before image-meta extract
(brightness windows, $B{=}40$). RF flat ($\approx 0.03$);
$\rho_{\mathrm{PO}}^{\mathrm{image}}(\mathrm{mask}/\mathrm{full})\approx 1.95$.
The \emph{same} image meta dims (brightness-led) lose nuisance quality
under patch ablation $\Rightarrow$ PO-risk up, power unchanged.

\section{Takeaway}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Which dimension}: CFPerm block power picks
        \texttt{spacy\_syntax} (syntax), \texttt{vader\_sent}/text\_meta
        (sentiment), \texttt{embed\_pca} (heldout residual).
  \item \textbf{Which meta-features}: VADER
        \{\texttt{compound},\texttt{neg},\texttt{polar\_gap},\texttt{neu}\}
        for emotion; \{\texttt{verb\_noun\_ratio}, POS\} for syntax;
        \{\texttt{img\_brightness},\texttt{contrast},\texttt{sat}\} for
        vision.
  \item \textbf{$\rho_{\mathrm{PO}}$}: absorb ($0.25$/$0.19$/$0.62$) vs
        residual ($\approx 1$) tells whether those features ate the
        channel or left concept in $Z$.
  \item Digested $X{=}[Z,M]$: crude $M$ still lifts CF / absorbs polarity
        on top of $Z$---\textbf{embedding does not represent everything}.
\end{enumerate}
