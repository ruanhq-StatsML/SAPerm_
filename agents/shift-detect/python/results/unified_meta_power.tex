\begin{abstract}
\noindent
One software path---digest $X=[Z_{\mathrm{PCA}},M]$ with named block
indices, then RFPerm / DRPerm / CFPerm---produces three empirical facts:
(i)~meta raises power when the alternative sits in structure;
(ii)~the joint $(\Delta\mathrm{Power},\rho_{\mathrm{PO}},\Delta\mathrm{CF})$
pattern is a \emph{directional interaction signature}
(absorb / localize / concept residual), not a repair diagnostic;
(iii)~the same block machinery ports to cross-modality CFPerm and to
ModaSense text$\leftrightarrow$image. Feature construction (spaCy+VADER)
is treated as a fixed POC input; this note reports results only.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setup (one line)}
BiasFinder BERT $\to Z_{\mathrm{PCA}}$ ($k{=}24$); spaCy+VADER $\to M$
($p_M{=}37$); schema list-indices separate \texttt{embed\_pca},
\texttt{vader\_sent}, \texttt{spacy\_syntax}, \ldots\
Digestion: \texttt{datasets/biasfinder\_gen.py}.
\[
\rho_{\mathrm{PO}}=\frac{\mathrm{PO}(Z{+}M)}{\mathrm{PO}(Z)},\quad
\mathrm{PO}=\mathbb{E}[\tau(X)^2],\
\tau\approx(Y-\mu)(W-e).
\]

\section{Directional signatures (BiasFinder; $B{=}150$)}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lcccc}
\toprule
Scenario & $\rho_{\mathrm{PO}}$ & $\Delta$RF & $\Delta$CF & Signature \\
\midrule
null\_shift & 1.07 & $0.00$ & $+0.03$ & null calibration \\
sentiment\_meta & \textbf{0.25} & $+0.06$ & $+0.01$ & \textbf{absorb} \\
syntax\_meta HARD & \textbf{0.19} & $-0.04$ & $\mathbf{+0.09}$ & \textbf{localize} \\
train\_vs\_heldout & 1.03 & $0.00$ & $\mathbf{+0.23}$ & concept residual \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Absorb} ($\rho\ll 1$, DR$\approx 0$): $M$ enters $(\mu,e)$ and
        removes the emotion covariate channel---interaction as nuisance,
        not a louder alarm. Digest subset names
        \texttt{vader\_compound}/\texttt{neg}/\texttt{polar\_gap}/\texttt{neu}.
  \item \textbf{Localize} ($\Delta\mathrm{CF}>0$): CFPerm names the meta
        block even when PO is absorbed (syntax HARD $+0.09$).
  \item \textbf{Concept residual} ($\rho\approx 1$, RF/DR$=1$): embedding
        still carries the shift; meta does not replace $Z$, but CF $+0.23$
        still attributes.
\end{itemize}

\section{Cross-modality block CFPerm}
Same windows; LOCO on \texttt{embed\_pca}, full \texttt{meta},
\texttt{vader\_sent}, \texttt{spacy\_syntax}, \texttt{spacy\_style}
($B{=}150$ for null/sentiment/syntax; heldout $B{=}30$ partial, pattern stable).
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lcccccc}
\toprule
Scenario & $\rho$ & embed & meta & vader & syntax & style \\
\midrule
null & 1.05 & 0.05 & 0.07 & 0.04 & 0.09 & 0.04 \\
sentiment\_meta & \textbf{0.25} & 0.01 & 0.03 & 0.02 & 0.04 & 0.06 \\
syntax\_meta HARD & \textbf{0.19} & 0.16 & \textbf{0.31} & 0.18 & \textbf{0.32} & 0.20 \\

\end{center}
Syntax HARD: CF concentrates on \texttt{meta}/\texttt{spacy\_syntax}
($0.31$/$0.32$) above embed ($0.16$)---named localization matches the
routing axis. Sentiment: $\rho{=}0.25$ absorb with quiet CF (channel
removed, not re-alarmed). Heldout: embed and meta both near ceiling;
residual is concept-level, meta still co-localizes.

\section{Takeaway}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Meta effects are large where structure is the alternative
        (feature-only $\Delta{+}0.60$/$+0.90$; RQ $+0.47$).
  \item Direction is the scientific object:
        absorb ($\rho{\approx}0.25$), localize ($\Delta\mathrm{CF}{+}0.09$),
        residual ($\rho{\approx}1$, CF${+}0.23$).
  \item Cross-modality CFPerm and ModaSense reuse the same indices:
        the block that should light up, does.
\end{enumerate}
