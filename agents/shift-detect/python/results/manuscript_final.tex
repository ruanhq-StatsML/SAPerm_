\begin{abstract}
\noindent
We study distribution-shift detection and localization with a portable
digestion $\to$ interaction $\to$ subset pipeline.
The primary objects are RFPerm / DRPerm / CFPerm power (Random Forest
outcome), modality MMD share $\pi_m$, the PO-risk ratio
$\rho_{\mathrm{PO}}$, and packaged CFPerm LOCO $\to$ feature-subset
localization.
Across feature-only DGPs, BiasFinder embeddings with named meta, and
ModaSense multimodal emotion~\cite{modasense_emotion_2026}, the same
read applies: MMD marks the moving channel, power decides whether the
shift is rejectable, $\rho_{\mathrm{PO}}$ decides absorb vs residual, and
localization names executable features---not anonymous PCA dims.
A closing empirical chapter reports the full ModaSense method comparison
($B{=}100$, $N_{\mathrm{perm}}{=}150$) under random split and concept drift.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setup}
%% ------------------------------------------------------------------
Digestion returns a typed object $X$ with named blocks:
\[
X \;=\;
\begin{cases}
[Z_{\mathrm{PCA}},\,M] & \text{BiasFinder}\\[2pt]
[M_t,M_i,M_a] & \text{ModaSense}
\end{cases}
\]
Software surface: \texttt{digest} / \texttt{interactions} /
\texttt{subset\_localize} / \texttt{run\_benchmark\_all}.
Localization is the packaged CFPerm block LOCO followed by
\texttt{subset\_localize} (no new method).

\[
\rho_{\mathrm{PO}}=\frac{\mathrm{PO}(X_{\mathrm{rich}})}{\mathrm{PO}(X_{\mathrm{base}})},
\quad
\pi_m=\frac{\mathrm{MMD}_m}{\sum_{m'}\mathrm{MMD}_{m'}}.
\]

\section{ModaSense interpretation}
\label{sec:modasense}
%% ------------------------------------------------------------------
On ModaSense/emotion ($n{=}500$, designed pairwise label conflict),
routing a batch gap along one modality and reading
RF/CF/$\pi$/$\rho_{\mathrm{PO}}$ together is unusually interpretable.
The text-sentiment window closes
($\pi_t{=}0.91$, RF${=}1$, CF$_t{=}1$, VADER subset, $\rho{=}1.95$).
The image-brightness window is silent absorb
($\pi_i{=}0.95$, $\rho{=}0.62$, RF/CF near $\alpha$).
Audio remains MMD-only.
When the chain closes, packaged localization returns executable names
(e.g.\ \texttt{vader\_neg}/\texttt{compound}/\texttt{polar\_gap}),
not anonymous coordinates.

\paragraph{Protocol.}

\section{Takeaway}
%% ------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Digestion + interactions + packaged subset localization is enough
        software.
  \item $\rho_{\mathrm{PO}}$ separates absorb from residual; CFPerm names
        the interacting block; \texttt{subset\_localize} names features.
  \item Embeddings are incomplete for shift work: crude meta still moves
        power, absorption, and localization on top of $Z$.
  \item ModaSense makes the multimodal story readable: closed chain on
        text, silent absorb on image, MMD-only on audio (pending full
        $B{=}100$ table in Section~\ref{sec:fullpower}).
\end{enumerate}
