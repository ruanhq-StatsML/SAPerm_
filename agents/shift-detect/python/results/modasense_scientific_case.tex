\begin{abstract}
\noindent
On ModaSense/emotion we report four quantities jointly:
RFPerm power, CFPerm block power, modality MMD share
$\pi_m=\mathrm{MMD}_m/\sum_{m'}\mathrm{MMD}_{m'}$, and
$\rho_{\mathrm{PO}}=\mathrm{PO}([M_t,M_i])/\mathrm{PO}(M_t)$,
plus carrier/LOCO localization when supported by CF.
Numbers are from $B{=}80$ power runs and $B{=}50$ MMD sweeps; we do not
upgrade weak CF/audio evidence into strong claims.
\end{abstract}

\section{Findings}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Text window closes the chain.}
        $\pi_{\mathrm{text}}{=}0.91$, RFPerm${=}1$, CFPerm text${=}1$,
        subset $=\{\texttt{vader\_neg},\texttt{compound},\texttt{pos},
        \texttt{polar\_gap},\texttt{neu}\}$, $\rho{=}1.95$
        (saturated detect, not absorb).
  \item \textbf{Image window: silent absorb.}
        $\pi_{\mathrm{image}}{=}0.95$ and $\rho{=}0.62$, but RF/CF near $\alpha$.
        MMD/$\rho_{\mathrm{PO}}$ track a covariate channel; they are not
        substitutes for rejection power or block localization.
  \item \textbf{Audio window incomplete.}
        $\pi_{\mathrm{audio}}{=}0.95$ only; RF/CF/$\rho$ do not yet close.
        No localization claim.
  \item \textbf{Protocol.}
        Declare a modality as the driver only when MMD share, RFPerm,
        CFPerm, and (when relevant) $\rho_{\mathrm{PO}}$ agree; otherwise
        report the split explicitly.
\end{enumerate}
