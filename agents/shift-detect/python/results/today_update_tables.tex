\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Two deliverables from this update.
(1)~ModaSense joint evidence:
RFPerm / CFPerm power, modality MMD share $\pi_m$, $\rho_{\mathrm{PO}}$,
and localization---reported only when the chain closes.
(2)~A Digested-object insight on BiasFinder:
even crude named meta ($M$) still moves power / absorption / CF localization
on top of BERT PCA ($Z$).
That is direct evidence that \textbf{embeddings do not represent everything}
relevant to shift detection.
\end{abstract}

\paragraph{Findings (ModaSense).}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
  \item Text window closes the chain:
        $\pi_t{=}0.91$, RF${=}1$, CF$_t{=}1$, VADER subset, $\rho{=}1.95$.
  \item Image window is \emph{silent absorb}:
        $\pi_i{=}0.95$ and $\rho{=}0.62$, but RF/CF near $\alpha$.
        MMD/$\rho_{\mathrm{PO}}$ track a covariate channel; they are not
        substitutes for rejection power.
  \item Audio remains MMD-only under current $B$/Y; no localization claim.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Digested insight: embedding $\neq$ everything}
\label{sec:embed}
%% ------------------------------------------------------------------
Digestion returns a typed object
\[
\texttt{Digested}:\quad
X \;=\;
\begin{cases}
[Z_{\mathrm{PCA}},\,M] & \text{BiasFinder (embed + text meta)}\\[2pt]
[M_t,M_i,M_a] & \text{ModaSense (meta-only multimodal)}
\end{cases}
\]
with named \texttt{schema.blocks} / \texttt{schema.modality} for CF LOCO.
Meta builders (spaCy+VADER, OpenCV, WAV stats) are intentionally simple---
feature quality is ``just OK.'' The point is not that $M$ is fancy; it is
that \textbf{$Z$ alone leaves measurable gaps}.

\paragraph{Insight.}
Even crude named meta still moves $\rho_{\mathrm{PO}}$, CF, and
feature-only power on top of BERT PCA---so
\textbf{embedding does not represent everything}.

\paragraph{ModaSense in one paragraph.}
On ModaSense/emotion~\cite{modasense_emotion_2026} ($n{=}500$,
designed pairwise label conflict), $X{=}[M_t,M_i,M_a]$ with
RF/CF/$\pi$/$\rho_{\mathrm{PO}}$ yields an interpretable trichotomy:
text-sentiment closes the chain
($\pi_t{=}0.91$, RF${=}1$, CF$_t{=}1$, VADER subset, $\rho{=}1.95$);
image-brightness is silent absorb
($\pi_i{=}0.95$, $\rho{=}0.62$, power${\approx}\alpha$);
audio remains MMD-only. Closed chains name executable features, not
anonymous dims.
