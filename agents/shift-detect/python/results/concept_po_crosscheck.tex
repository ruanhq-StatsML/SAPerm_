\begin{abstract}
\noindent
We argue that power change and PO-risk ratio are not rival metrics.
Under controlled concept / covariate DGPs, their \emph{joint} signature
with vs without meta-features is the cross-validation of embed$\times$meta
interaction---and it must match the known data-generating process.
\end{abstract}

\section{Quantities}
\[
\rho_{\mathrm{PO}}=\frac{\mathrm{PO}(Z{+}M)}{\mathrm{PO}(Z)},
\quad
\mathrm{PO}=\mathbb{E}[\tau(X)^2],\
\tau\approx(Y-\mu(X))(W-e(X)).
\]
$\Delta\mathrm{Power}$: rejection rate on embed+meta minus embed-only
(RFPerm / DRPerm). $\Delta\mathrm{CF}$: CFPerm power on the meta block
minus a size-matched embed placebo.

\section{Synthetic DGPs (known truth)}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
DGP & Mechanism & Predicted signature \\
\midrule
\texttt{null} & no shift & Type-I $\approx\alpha$; $\rho\approx 1$ \\
\texttt{covariate\_meta} & $M$ shifts; $P(Y\mid X)$ fixed &
  $\rho_{\mathrm{PO}}\ll 1$, DR power stays low (\textbf{absorb}) \\
\texttt{concept\_embed} & $P(Y\mid Z)$ changes; $M$ irrelevant &
  $\rho_{\mathrm{PO}}\approx 1$, DR high both sides (\textbf{residual}) \\
\texttt{concept\_meta} & $P(Y\mid M)$ changes across batch &
  DR high with meta; $\Delta\mathrm{CF}>0$ (\textbf{localize}) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\section{Why this is cross-validation}
If meta truly interacts with the shift channel, the triple
$(\Delta\mathrm{Power},\,\rho_{\mathrm{PO}},\,\Delta\mathrm{CF})$ must
land in the predicted cell of the table above.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Absorb without power rise is \emph{success} under covariate\_meta.
  \item Power high and $\rho\approx 1$ under concept\_embed means meta
        cannot invent a false absorb story.
  \item Localize under concept\_meta means CFPerm names the interacting
        coordinates while PO still flags concept.
\end{itemize}
Mismatch would falsify the interpretation; match is positive evidence
that power and PO-risk \emph{jointly} read interaction.
